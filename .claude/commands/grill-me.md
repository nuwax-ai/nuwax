# Grill Me: Decision-Tree Interview

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of **$ARGUMENTS** until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer based on best practices.

Ask the questions **one at a time** — do not batch them.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase (checking existing patterns, architecture, or constraints), explore the codebase instead of guessing.

Continue until all decision branches are resolved or I signal to stop.
